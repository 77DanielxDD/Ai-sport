However, traditional agricultural sales models face issues such as information lag, disorganized inventory management, and low product circulation efficiency. There is a need for an efficient and intelligent management system to improve the sales and circulation of agricultural products. This paper designs and implements an Agricultural Product Mall Management System, which integrates the needs of three roles: consumers, merchants, and administrators, providing a unified online platform to optimize the sales and management processes of agricultural products. The system uses the Spring Boot framework for the backend, combined with MyBatis and MySQL databases, while the frontend is built using the Vue.js framework, and the user interface is presented through WeChat Mini Program. Consumers can easily browse products, place orders, make payments, evaluate products, and apply for after-sales services; merchants can release product information, manage inventory, process orders, and interact with consumers; administrators are responsible for user management, product review, and order processing. The system improves product circulation efficiency and enhances user experience through features like intelligent inventory management, data analysis, and personalized recommendations. Through the design, development, and testing of the system, this study validates its effectiveness in improving agricultural product sales, optimizing management processes, and promoting agricultural informatization.
